Currently, I am a PhD student at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Moin Qureshi in the Future Architecture and Systems Lab. Previously, I was a Research Fellow at Microsoft for a little short of 2 years.

I engage in systems for ML and computer architecture research.

What I Do

Research Interests

    My research focuses on enabling hardware-efficient AI inference through the mediums of algorithms, systems and computer architecture.

Work Experience

  • Research Intern, Microsoft Research, Cambridge, UK May 2026 – Aug 2026
    • Fault tolerance for disaggregated decode: the impact of network link and device failures on decode throughput and tail latencies.
    • Developed cost-effective scale-out network topologies for disaggregated inference, resilient to network failures at datacenter scale.
  • Research Fellow, Microsoft Research, Bangalore, India Jan 2024 – Jul 2025
    • Developed LeanAttention, a hardware-aware scalable attention execution mechanism that accelerates attention by 2.6x over FlashAttention-2
    • Published research paper and filed patents on novel techniques to improve the efficiency of LLM inference.
